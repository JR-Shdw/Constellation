# Support and funding

Constellation is funded by donations. There is no paid tier of the software,
and there never will be.

## Always free, to everyone

- The full source, under Apache-2.0.
- Every release and every pre-built artifact.
- Every security fix, published at the same time to everyone.
- The documentation.

**No release, build, update or security fix is ever restricted to donors,
sponsors or customers, nor given to them earlier.** This is a standing
commitment, not a current state of affairs. If you can read this repository,
you have the same access as the people funding it.

That line matters for more than fairness. Under the EU Cyber Resilience Act,
donation-funded software stays outside the regulation's scope only so long as
donating is not in practice a condition for access to the software, its
essential functions, or its updates (Commission guidance on Regulation (EU)
2024/2847, and recital 15 on commercial activity). Gating builds or patches
behind sponsorship would change the project's legal status, not just its
reputation.

## Community support

Open an issue. Best effort, no response time, no service commitment.

The software is provided "AS IS", without warranties or conditions of any
kind. See Apache-2.0 sections 7 and 8, and the NOTICE file.

## Deployment assistance

Hands-on help with deployment, onboarding, integration or day-two operations
may be arranged, and may be reserved for people funding the project.

This is a service: it is time, not access. It is not part of the Apache-2.0
grant, it carries no warranty, and declining to fund the project costs you
nothing in terms of software, updates or fixes.

Contact: shdw <contact@resurgamus.com>

## Donations

Donations are accepted without the intention of making a profit. They offset
the cost of building and maintaining the project, including the maintainer's
time.

Donations buy no rights over the project, no influence over its roadmap, and
no privileged access. See `.github/FUNDING.yml` for the current channels.

## AI and machine-learning training

Not covered by donations or by the Apache-2.0 grant. A text-and-data-mining
reservation is declared under article 4 of EU directive 2019/790; see
LICENSE-AI.md.
