## Scope of the reservation

Be clear about the mechanism. The Apache License 2.0 grants broad rights to
**use, modify and redistribute the software**, including commercially, and it
contains no field-of-use restriction. This policy therefore does **not** claim
that the copyright license withholds machine-learning rights.

What is withheld is consent under the text-and-data-mining reservation
declared above, which is a statutory mechanism under article 4 of EU directive
2019/790 and operates independently of the copyright license. Consent is not
given for any of the following, and a separate written agreement is required:

1. **Pre-training** of large language models, code models, or any other
   neural networks where the codebase, in whole or in part, becomes part
   of the training corpus.
2. **Fine-tuning** of pre-trained models using this codebase as additional
   training data, supervised examples, or RLHF reference output.
3. **Evaluation / benchmarking** of models where the codebase is used as
   a held-out evaluation set without licensing arrangement.
4. **Distillation** — using outputs derived from this codebase (such as
   model completions about it) to train a downstream model.
5. **Embedding / retrieval-augmented generation (RAG)** at commercial
   scale where the codebase is indexed and served as model context to
   third parties.
6. **Synthetic data generation** based on the structure or substance of
   this codebase.

Personal, academic, and incidental research uses (single-user IDE
assistants, classroom analysis, code review tools used by the legitimate
licensee) are not the target of this reservation. The line is **commercial
or large-scale ML training and serving**.
